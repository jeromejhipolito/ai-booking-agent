# Studio Kalye — front-desk knowledge base

Everything the agent is allowed to say about the salon. One `##` section per chunk; the
heading becomes the chunk's stable slug, so editing a section updates it in place on
re-ingest instead of duplicating it.

**If a customer asks something this file does not answer, the agent must say it does not
know and offer to have a human follow up.** Nothing here is a suggestion to the model — it
is the entire set of facts it may state.

## Opening hours

Studio Kalye is open Tuesday to Sunday. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday we are open 9:00am
to 6:00pm. Friday and Saturday we are open 9:00am to 8:00pm. Sunday we are open 10:00am to
5:00pm. We are closed every Monday. Individual stylists keep their own days off, so a
specific stylist may not be available even on a day the salon is open.

## Where we are

We are at 214 Kalye Uno, Barangay Sikatuna Village, Quezon City. We are a five-minute walk
from the Kalye Uno jeepney stop. The salon is on the second floor above the bakery; there is
a lift at the back of the building.

## Parking

There is free street parking along Kalye Uno, and a paid lot two doors down that charges 50
pesos for the first three hours. On Saturdays the street fills up by about 10am, so the
paid lot is the safer bet.

## What we charge

A haircut is 450 pesos and takes about 45 minutes. Blow dry and style is 350 pesos and takes
30 minutes. A manicure is 300 pesos and takes 45 minutes. A hair spa treatment is 900 pesos
and takes an hour. Full-head hair colour is 2,500 pesos and takes about two hours. Rebonding
is 4,500 pesos and takes around four hours. Prices are per person and already include the
wash and finish.

## How to pay

We accept cash, GCash and Maya. We do not take credit or debit cards. Payment is settled at
the front desk after your service, not when you book — booking through chat costs nothing
and takes no deposit.

## Cancelling or moving an appointment

You can cancel or move an appointment any time up to two hours before it starts, and there
is no fee. Inside two hours we ask that you call the salon so we can try to fill the slot.
We do not charge a no-show fee, but three no-shows in a row means future bookings need a
phone confirmation.

## Booking with a specific stylist

You are welcome to ask for a particular stylist, and we will book them if they are free at
the time you want. If they are not, we will say so plainly and offer you their next opening
or another stylist. Every stylist at Studio Kalye is trained to the same standard, and we
will never move you to someone else without asking you first.

## Rebonding — what to expect

Rebonding takes about four hours, so we only start one before 2pm. Come with dry, unwashed
hair — washing it the same morning makes the treatment take less well. Do not tie your hair
up or wash it for three days afterwards. We recommend a hair spa about two weeks later to
settle the ends. Rebonding is done by Maria only.

## After a colour service

Wait at least 48 hours before your first wash, and use a sulphate-free shampoo after that.
Keep coloured hair out of chlorinated pools for the first two weeks, or wear a cap. Colour
usually needs a refresh every six to eight weeks depending on how fast your roots grow.

## Walk-ins

We take walk-ins when there is a gap, but Friday and Saturday are usually fully booked, so a
walk-in is likely to be turned away on those days. Booking ahead through this chat is the
only way to hold a slot.

## Bringing children

Children are welcome. We cut children's hair at the same price as an adult haircut. We do not
have a dedicated play area, so a child waiting for a parent is happier with something to do.

## Our stylists

Maria Santos is our senior colourist, with twelve years in colour correction and balayage,
trained in Seoul. Joy Ramirez is a senior stylist who does precision cuts and keratin
treatments. Ruel Bautista is our master barber, fifteen years on the chair, and does classic
barbering, fades and beard work. Bea Cruz is a stylist covering blow-dry styling, hair spa
and nail care.

## Allergies and sensitive scalps

Tell us before a colour or rebonding service if you have had a reaction to hair products
before. We can patch-test 48 hours ahead at no charge. We do not offer a formaldehyde-free
rebonding alternative at this branch.
